FAQ

Good questions

What if my children won't sit still?

They don't need to. Sessions are child-led and built around play — some of the best photos happen when nobody's looking at the camera.

When should I book a newborn session?

Ideally during pregnancy, so we can pencil in your due date. Sessions usually take place in the first few weeks after birth.

What should we wear?

Comfortable clothes in soft, coordinating colours (not matching). We'll send a short style guide once you've booked.

How do I pay?

We email an invoice for the booking fee to secure your date, and the balance is due before the session. You can pay by bank transfer or card.

What if it rains?

We'll keep an eye on the forecast together and reschedule outdoor sessions free of charge if needed.

Will you use our photos on your website?

Only if you say yes. We always ask for written permission before sharing any images, and especially images of children.
